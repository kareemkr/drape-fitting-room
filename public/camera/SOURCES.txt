Drape studio motion sample

studio-motion.mp4 is a silent, six-second animation of Omar, the original synthetic
adult male portrait from this project (public/studio/male-base.webp). We used the
portrait's own body landmarks to animate a weight shift, arm movement and a
slight change of stance. The camera fitting room detects the body from the
decoded video pixels. No pose data or clothing overlay is embedded in the clip.

Format: H.264, 512 × 768 pixels, 24 frames per second.

The three-panel motion comparison uses this same clip, with measured body points and the Oxford shirt drawn by the live renderer.

waist-poses.mp4 sequences four original synthetic portraits of two adults. Each
pose holds for three seconds. It contains only the input portraits. The app
detects the pose and fits the selected garment during playback.
waist-fit-comparison.jpg compares the previous and current local renderer
using the same original source portraits and the same product image.

size-comparison.jpg shows the Oxford shirt, open denim jacket and floral dress
rendered at their reference size and two estimated size increases. All panels
use synthetic studio photos and the on-device fitting renderer.
