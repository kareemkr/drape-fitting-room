# Visual evaluation

A reviewer compares the supplied portrait, separate garment image and cropped generated result for the same trial. This is a qualitative inspection at the available image resolution.

- **Wearer likeness.** The pictured person should remain recognisable by their facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, build and pose. Changes in styling or framing alone may be acceptable. Mark review for a readily visible identity, body shape, facial characteristic, or posture mismatch.
- **Garment.** Compare dominant and accent colours, silhouette, neckline, sleeves, closures and distinctive product details. Mark review if any clearly visible defining detail changes or disappears. Do not suggest that printing, pattern repeat, or sizing was independently measured.
- **Plausibility.** Check drape and folds around neckline, arms, torso and waist, body continuity, hands where visible, and occlusion with lower clothing. Mark review for visible artifacts or anatomically/physically implausible fit. Mark review when too little of the subject or garment is visible to evaluate.
- **Overall.** Pass when the reviewer marked all three criteria pass. Review means at least one targeted inspection identified a concern. A passing image is not evidence of real-life size or fit.
